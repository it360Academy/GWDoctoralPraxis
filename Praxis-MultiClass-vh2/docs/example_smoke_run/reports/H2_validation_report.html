H2: A Diagnostic Exclusivity Violation (DEV) occurs when a hospitalization's principal diagnosis (I10_DX1) co-occurs with an ICD-10-CM Excludes1-incompatible secondary diagnosis (DX2-DX25). Using the CMS Excludes1 standard as a research audit tool, AI/ML methods, and negative controls, this tests whether DEVs occur at measurable and reproducible rates across NIS 2021-2023.

H2: NOT SUPPORTED (compliance reading)
